The data on this site was last updated from a list maintained by the Department of Neighborhoods, retrieved via public records request in March 2025. If your civic association is not listed on this website and you think it should be, please contact [the City of Columbus Department of Neighborhoods](https://www.columbus.gov/Community/Department-of-Neighborhoods) so that they're aware of both your association and this list.

This website is a personal hobby project by [Ben Keith](https://benlk.com/), a Columbus resident who finds the City's websites and communications inadequate for many purposes.

To contribute to the development of this site, [refer to the GitHub repository](https://github.com/benlk/columbus-civic-associations).

For similar projects maintained by Ben Keith, see [this list](https://benlk.github.io/).
